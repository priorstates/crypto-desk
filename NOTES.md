## Goal

I research and trade crypto on Coinbase. Build my desk: a recorder for BTC-USD and ETH-USD from Coinbase's public market data feed, running around the clock on my server trade1; a paper market-making strategy on BTC-USD that quotes around the mid and logs its simulated fills, position and PnL; a risk monitor with position and loss limits that alerts me when the feed goes stale or a limit is hit; and a research notebook that studies spreads, volatility and how prices move after trades, using the recorded data. Paper trading only: no real orders.
